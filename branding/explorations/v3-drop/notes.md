# Drop into the slot

Three desktop slots side by side. The middle app has dropped into its slot, sitting lower than its neighbours, with a small chevron above that shows the motion. Orchid accent.

Palette: graphite #16181d; near white #f4f5f7; paper #f4f3f0; accent #f27fd8; deep accent #a02b88. WCAG sRGB contrast: accent on graphite 7.44:1; deep on paper 5.92:1 (both at least 4.5:1). Deep replaces the accent in light icons and lockups.

Own silhouette: Three upright columns with one lowered, not horizontal pills (OmaBar), a square ring (omagnet), two square tiles (omahop) or arcs (omadials).

At 16 px: Columns are 4 units wide with 1 unit gaps. The lowered middle column changes the silhouette, so the idea survives in one colour. The chevron is a 1.4 unit stroke.

Typeface: lowercase omaplace in JetBrains Mono Bold, outlined with fontTools. All icons are transparent; icon.svg is for dark, icon-light.svg for light, icon-mono.svg is graphite and icon-mono-dark.svg is near white. Wordmarks and lockups follow the same surface naming.

Construction: original shapes on a 16 by 16 grid. PNG exports and the 1024 px concept sheet are rendered from those real vectors with librsvg. No generated images, embedded raster masters, gradients, shadows or glow.
