# The desktop plan

A monitor drawn as a desktop plan: one tall zone and two stacked zones, on a short stand. The lower right zone is filled by an app that has landed, in azure and inset from its zone.

Palette: graphite #16181d; near white #f4f5f7; paper #f4f3f0; accent #5aa9ff; deep accent #1f5fae. WCAG sRGB contrast: accent on graphite 7.23:1; deep on paper 5.72:1 (both at least 4.5:1). Deep replaces the accent in light icons and lockups.

Own silhouette: Unequal zones on a monitor stand, not a square ring of four equal quarters (omagnet), two tiles under an arc (omahop), pills on a rail (OmaBar) or a corner frame with arcs (omadials).

At 16 px: All shapes sit on whole units with 1 unit gaps. The landed zone is inset by 1 unit on every side, so it reads as a separate block even in one colour.

Typeface: lowercase omaplace in JetBrains Mono Bold, outlined with fontTools. All icons are transparent; icon.svg is for dark, icon-light.svg for light, icon-mono.svg is graphite and icon-mono-dark.svg is near white. Wordmarks and lockups follow the same surface naming.

Construction: original shapes on a 16 by 16 grid. PNG exports and the 1024 px concept sheet are rendered from those real vectors with librsvg. No generated images, embedded raster masters, gradients, shadows or glow.
