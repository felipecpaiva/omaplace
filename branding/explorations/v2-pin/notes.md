# Lands here

A map pin with a small window cut into its head, its point landing on a slot. The pin is the app and the slot is where it goes. Lime pin, near white slot.

Palette: graphite #16181d; near white #f4f5f7; paper #f4f3f0; accent #a6e05a; deep accent #4a7a12. WCAG sRGB contrast: accent on graphite 11.36:1; deep on paper 4.63:1 (both at least 4.5:1). Deep replaces the accent in light icons and lockups.

Own silhouette: A single teardrop pin above a bar. No grid, no tiles, no arcs, no corner frame. Nothing in the family uses a pin or a cut-out window.

At 16 px: The head is 10 units wide and the window hole is 6 by 3, so the cut-out stays open at 16 px. A 1 unit gap separates the pin point from the slot.

Typeface: lowercase omaplace in JetBrains Mono Bold, outlined with fontTools. All icons are transparent; icon.svg is for dark, icon-light.svg for light, icon-mono.svg is graphite and icon-mono-dark.svg is near white. Wordmarks and lockups follow the same surface naming.

Construction: original shapes on a 16 by 16 grid. PNG exports and the 1024 px concept sheet are rendered from those real vectors with librsvg. No generated images, embedded raster masters, gradients, shadows or glow.
