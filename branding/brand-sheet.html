omaplace puts every app back where it belongs on Omarchy: placement rules, desktop plans, named scenes and session restore. The mark: a monitor drawn as a desktop plan of unequal zones, one app landed in azure. Every app in its zone.

Text uses Near white, Graphite and the two Muted greys (AA or better). Azure and Azure deep mark the landed app. The approved azure fails on light grounds (Azure #5aa9ff on #f4f3f0 is 2.21:1; Azure #5aa9ff on #ffffff is 2.46:1), so light files use Azure deep. No gradients, shadows or glows in the mark.

3. Type

JetBrains Mono

Bold for headings and the wordmark, regular for body. It is Omarchy's default font (SIL OFL licence), so omaplace looks at home on the desktop it targets. Fallback: any monospace.

The wordmark omaplace is JetBrains Mono Bold converted to outlines, one colour. One word, all lower case. Say it "oh-ma-place".

4. Spacing and shape

  • The mark lives on a 16 unit grid: a tall zone 7 by 10, a zone 6 by 4, the landed app 4 by 3 inset 1 unit inside its zone, a stand 6 by 2. Gaps of at least 1 unit.
  • Base unit 8 px. Card padding 8% of the width, gaps 16 or 24 px.
  • Clear space around the mark: 2 grid units (an eighth of the icon) on every side.
  • Minimum size: 16 px for the icon (icon-small), 140 px wide for the lockup.
  • Gently rounded zones (radius 1 unit), the stand fully round. Icon tile radius 22% of its width.

5. Do and do not

  • Use the graphite tile icon for app, favicon and avatar slots, the glyph on flat grounds.
  • Use the light files (*-light.*, Azure deep) on light pages.
  • Write the name as omaplace: one word, all lower case.
  • Keep the not-affiliated line near any mention of Omarchy.
  • Keep text short and plain.
  • Recolour, stretch or rotate the mark, or add a glow, shadow or gradient.
  • Put the light azure on a light ground, or set body text in either azure.
  • Use Omarchy's logo, or any styling that reads as an official Omarchy mark.
  • Write Omaplace, OmaPlace or oma place.
  • Use an em dash or en dash in any text.
