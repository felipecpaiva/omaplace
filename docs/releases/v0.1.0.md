# omaplace v0.1.0

Every app opens where it belongs on Omarchy. Plan each desktop on one page, save scenes, and bring the whole session back after a reboot.

![The Desktops page: a 2 column plan, then Open all](https://github.com/felipecpaiva/omaplace/releases/download/v0.1.0/desktops.gif)

What is in it:

- **Desktops page.** One card per desktop on each monitor, with a badge for what it is now or what its plan is. Pick a layout (2 or 3 columns, 2 rows, a 2 by 2 grid, Priority, your own zones as text, Tiled, Scrolling, Full screen, Free floating) and an app per zone. "Open all" starts what is missing and moves what is open. A planned app opened by hand at any time goes to its zone. Move a planned window by hand and one notification offers "Update plan". "Open at login" per desktop, off by default.

![A planned app opened by hand goes to its zone](https://github.com/felipecpaiva/omaplace/releases/download/v0.1.0/snap.gif)

![Update plan: a window dragged to the other zone, then the two swap](https://github.com/felipecpaiva/omaplace/releases/download/v0.1.0/update.gif)

- **Scenes.** `omaplace save work` saves the desktop you are on; `omaplace open work` opens it again in one step. Exact places on a floating desktop, left to right order on a tiled one. Terminals come back in their folder with their program, and Chrome profiles go back to their own desktops.

![Save a scene, close its windows, open it again](https://github.com/felipecpaiva/omaplace/releases/download/v0.1.0/scenes.gif)

- **Session restore.** A snapshot of all desktops, written only after window events and at most once every 15 seconds. `omaplace restore` brings back the previous login, each window on its own monitor and desktop.

- **Placement rules.** "This app always opens on desktop 3 of this monitor", following the monitor across ports, off while that monitor is unplugged.
- **App memory.** An app opened by hand goes back to where a scene put it, or to where you last left it.
- **Settings window** with Desktops, Scenes, Rules, Session and General pages, and an optional Scenes menu in the Omarchy menu, added only when you press the button.

What was tested: 46 automated suites (Lua unit and fake Hyprland tests, Node tests, plugin validation, the scrub pass), all passing. Live on one laptop monitor with harmless apps: rules (slot, follow, removing the load line leaves Hyprland as before, nothing runs while idle), save and open of scenes on floating and tiled desktops, terminals with their program, two Chrome profiles, app memory, session snapshots and a restore from a previous session, the menu block on a copy of a real menu file, and the Desktops page: zones, Open all, snapping, zone swap and new plan through "Update plan", Full screen maximized with the bar kept.

Known limits: one monitor only so far (a port change and an unplugged rule monitor are not checked on real hardware). "Open at login" and a restore after a real new login are not checked yet. Until [omacom/omarchy#19671](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy/pull/19671) lands, switching a desktop's mode presses Omarchy's layout toggle on that desktop. The "Update plan" button was not pressed with a real click (the command behind it was run). No visual zone editor. A Chrome window opened from Chrome's own profile menu cannot be placed by profile. Hand checks are in `docs/validation-checklist.md`, next steps in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

How to install: see the README. Once the repo is public: `omarchy plugin add https://github.com/felipecpaiva/omaplace --enable`, then `omarchy restart shell` once, and add the one `pcall(dofile, ...)` line from the README to the end of `~/.config/hypr/hyprland.lua`.
