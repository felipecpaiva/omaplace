# omaplace v0.3.0

A real settings app: one window that stays open while you arrange your apps, with your desktops, apps, scenes and restore on five clear pages.

![The settings app: Desktops, Apps, Scenes, Restore and General](https://github.com/felipecpaiva/omaplace/releases/download/v0.3.0/settings-app.gif)

What is new:

- **The omaplace settings app.** A normal window that floats, stays open when you click outside it, and comes to the front when you open it again. Five pages: **Desktops** (a map of every desktop on every monitor, and the plan of the one you pick), **Apps** (where each app always opens, what apps you open yourself do, apps to leave alone), **Scenes**, **Restore** and **General**. Keys: Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+5 for the pages, F5 reads the desktops again, ? or F1 lists the keys. It is one optional package, `omaplace-settings` (below). The old settings panel of the plugin is gone, so there is only one place to change things.
- **Apps with the same name are told apart.** Two launcher entries called the same (a terminal app and a desktop app, for example) show a hint: "Pad (terminal)" and "Pad (pad-app)".

![Two apps with one name, and a note on a shared window class](https://github.com/felipecpaiva/omaplace/releases/download/v0.3.0/app-names.gif)

- **The layout list follows the desktop.** A tiled desktop offers "Tiled (now)" first. A floating desktop offers its own omagnet layout first, when omagnet has one for that desktop or monitor.

![The layout list on a tiled and on a floating desktop](https://github.com/felipecpaiva/omaplace/releases/download/v0.3.0/layout-picker.gif)

- **Draw zones** in the app when omagnet is not installed (with omagnet, "Edit in omagnet" opens its own editor).

![Drawing zones: pick a zone, split it](https://github.com/felipecpaiva/omaplace/releases/download/v0.3.0/draw-zones.gif)

- **A rule from a window.** On Apps, press "From a window..." and click the app's window within 4 seconds: its desktop, monitor and, on a floating desktop, its size and spot are saved.

What was fixed:

- A terminal app started by Omarchy's launcher (its window is named by the terminal, for example `TUI.tile`) was saved under the wrong name, so a plan never placed it. omaplace now reads the right name, and warns when several apps share it.
- A click outside the settings closed them.

How it was checked: 49 automated suites. The app has 15 offscreen tests that click every control against a stand-in for the plugin, including a refused change, a shell restart and keyboard focus. Live on a laptop with an external monitor: a read-only run against the real plugin, 15 real changes through it (with the settings files put back byte for byte after), and the real window (floating, centred, one instance).

Known limits: "From a window..." and the folder chooser were not checked by hand. The app is in English only for now.

How to update: `omarchy plugin update`, then `omarchy restart shell` once. Then install the settings app: download `omaplace-settings-0.3.0-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst` below and run `sudo pacman -U omaplace-settings-0.3.0-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst`. Open it from the launcher (omaplace) or with `omaplace settings`. New install: see the README.
